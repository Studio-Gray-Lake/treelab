Welcome to TreeLab

A free tech demo from Studio Gray Lake. Grow a tree on the Tree tab, plant a forest on the Forest tab.

Drag to turn, scroll to zoom. On the Forest tab, tick "walk among the trees" to walk through it. H hides the panels.

TreeLab is 3D and works best on a computer. On a touch screen, pinch to zoom.
